How it works

Governed AI workflow

  1. Patient grants scoped consent.
  2. Provider submits an inference request.
  3. System validates provider, subject, model and expiry.
  4. Non-clinical model generates a synthetic label.
  5. Cryptographic digests create an audit receipt.
